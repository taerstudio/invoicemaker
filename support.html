Your data

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded to a server — invoices, clients, products and company details never leave your iPhone or iPad. The practical consequence: deleting the app deletes your data, and it is not recoverable. Export a backup first if you plan to remove or replace the app.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Settings → Backup & restore and export. JSON keeps everything and can be imported back into the app; CSV is meant for spreadsheets and accounting software. You can export all of your data or just one business. Save the file somewhere durable — Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself.

Invoicing

Sending an invoice doesn't work

Apple Mail only appears if a mail account is set up in the system Mail app, and Gmail and Outlook only appear if those apps are installed. All three send the invoice with the PDF attached — Gmail and Outlook through the system share sheet, because their compose links cannot carry a file on their own. If no mail app is available, the share button exports the same PDF to Files, Messages, WhatsApp or anywhere else.

I'm not getting payment reminders

Reminders need two things: the toggle in Settings → Payment reminders, and notification permission for InvoiceMaker in the iOS Settings app. A reminder fires at 9 a.m. on the due date, and only for an invoice that still has a balance and whose due date is in the future. An invoice that is already overdue is not re-notified — it is shown in Expected Payments instead.

Can I change the app language, or the language of the PDF?

Both, and independently. Settings → Language changes the app; PDF language sets what new documents use, and each individual invoice can override it from its own editor. Twelve languages are supported.

Can I invoice from more than one business?

Yes, with Pro. Add businesses in the account area, then switch the active one. Invoices, receivables and recurring schedules belong to the active business; clients and products are shared across all of them.

Pro & subscriptions

What does Pro unlock?

  • No ads
  • Every PDF template and colour palette
  • Estimates, and converting an estimate into an invoice
  • Signatures on invoices and estimates
  • Billing from more than one business

Everything else — unlimited invoices, clients and products, PDF export, payment tracking, reminders and backups — is free.

I bought Pro but the app still shows me ads / locked features

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchases belong to the Apple Account rather than to the device, so a restore is also what you need after switching phones.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Refund requests also go through Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy & data

Your business records stay on your device. Ads (AdMob) and crash and usage statistics (Firebase) are the only things that talk to a server, and neither ever receives your invoices or clients — the full details are in the Privacy Policy.

You can turn off personalised advertising at any time from Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where local law requires a consent form, the app also offers Settings → Ad privacy settings to change your advertising consent.
