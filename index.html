InvoiceMaker

Invoice, send, get paid — from your phone.

InvoiceMaker turns a client and a few line items into a finished PDF invoice, emails it, and keeps track of what is still owed. No account to create, no subscription needed to send your first invoice.

How it works

01

Set up your business

Company details, logo, currency and tax — entered once, reused on every document.

02

Build the invoice

Pick a client, add saved products or free-text items, apply discounts, choose a template.

03

Send and follow up

Email the PDF or share it anywhere, then record payments and watch the balance close.

What it does

  • Invoices and estimates. Quote first, convert the accepted estimate into an invoice without retyping it.
  • Payments, not just documents. Partial payments, running balances and an Expected Payments view of what is due.
  • Payment reminders. A notification on the morning an invoice falls due.
  • Recurring invoices. Set a schedule once and let the same invoice go out on it.
  • PDF templates. Several layouts and colour palettes, with your logo and signature.
  • Your data, exportable. JSON for a full backup you can import again, CSV for your accountant.
  • 12 languages, and the PDF can be in a different one from the app.
  • Nothing leaves your device. There is no account and no server holding your records.
